# ORIANA — Brand Guidelines

**Document 01 of the Oriana Guideline Suite · Version 1.0 · July 2026**

These guidelines govern every expression of the Oriana brand — the name, the mark, the palette, the type, the imagery, and the standards that keep them coherent. They are written to be used: short enough to actually read, strict enough to keep one artist looking like a brand, and flexible enough to grow from a one-chair HDB studio to whatever Oriana becomes.

---

## 1. The Brand in One Page

**Name:** Oriana (oh-ree-AH-na) — from Latin roots meaning "the rising golden light." In literature, the beloved of Amadís in medieval romance and the poetic name Renaissance madrigalists gave Elizabeth I.

**What we are:** A private, one-client-at-a-time lash studio in Singapore.

**The idea:** The golden hour — the light in which everyone looks like themselves, only more so. Nothing added; the light simply finds what was already there.

**The promise:** Lashes people assume are yours.

**Primary tagline:** *Noticed, not announced.*

**Descriptor lockup:** ORIANA · Private Lash Studio

**Personality in five words:** Warm. Literary. Unhurried. Precise. Quiet.

**The one test every brand decision must pass:** *Would this feel at home in golden-hour light — warm, natural, unforced? If it shouts, it fails.*

## 2. Brand Architecture

Oriana is the brand; Chesa is the artist and the face. The brand carries the world (name, light, ritual, standards); the artist carries the trust (skill, honesty, the documented craft journey). Written expression follows one rule: **Oriana speaks in the first-person plural or the impersonal editorial voice; Chesa speaks as "I" only in artist-voice contexts** (Instagram captions signed –C., the About page, WhatsApp conversations). Never blur the two in a single sentence.

The naming system beneath the brand is the arc of light, and it is expandable without ever leaving the world:

| Layer | Name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Masterbrand | **Oriana** | Never abbreviated in formal lockups |
| Short form | **Ori** | Captions, watermarks, in-world nickname only — never on signage or legal documents |
| Signature set | **First Light** | The hero service; always capitalized, never translated into technical jargon in headlines |
| Volume set | **Full Gold** | |
| Lash lift + tint | **The Lift** | |
| Infill | **The Return** | Doubles as the retention vocabulary ("your Return window") |
| Aftercare retail | **The Keepsake Kit** | |
| Future bridal | **The Long Light** | Reserved |
| Future training arm | **The Atelier of Hours** | Reserved |

**Rule:** every world-name appears with its plain descriptor on first use in any surface ("First Light — the signature set"). Mystery is welcome; confusion is not.

## 3. The Wordmark

The logotype is **ORIANA** set in a high-contrast literary serif, all caps, with generous letterspacing (tracking of approximately +12% to +18% of point size). The length of the word is an asset when tracked wide: it reads as a masthead, not a label.

**Primary lockup:** ORIANA over the descriptor "Private Lash Studio" in the quiet sans, letterspaced wider than the wordmark, at roughly one-fifth the wordmark's cap height.

**Secondary mark (the glyph):** a low sun above a horizon line, drawn in a single stroke, doubling as an open eye with light rising behind the lash line. Used as watermark, label stamp, and favicon.

**Short-form mark:** "Ori" with the sun-point — for social watermarks and tight spaces only.

Clear space around the wordmark equals the cap height of the O on all sides. Minimum sizes: wordmark 24 px / 8 mm wide print equivalent; glyph 16 px. Never: stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients to the wordmark; set it in another typeface; place it over busy photography without a scrim of the ivory or umber.

## 4. Colour

The palette is golden-hour editorial: warm, placeless, restrained. Four colours, used with discipline — the accent is the *seasoning*, never the dish.

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary ground | **Ivory Hour** | #F6F0E6 | ~60% | Backgrounds, cards, packaging ground |
| Primary ink | **Umber** | #3B2F26 | ~30% | All body text, wordmark, line work |
| Accent | **Honey Gold** | #C9963F | ≤5% | One accent per surface: a rule line, a glyph, a single word — never large fills |
| Support | **Blush Sand** | #E4CDBA | ~5% | Secondary surfaces, dividers, table fills |

Accessibility rule: Umber on Ivory Hour passes WCAG AA for all text sizes; Honey Gold is **never** used for body text on ivory (insufficient contrast) — it may carry display-size numerals, rules, and the glyph only. Forbidden territory: pink-glitter, black-and-gold luxe, greige minimalism, dusk blues (the previous direction), pure white (#FFFFFF), and pure black (#000000).

## 5. Typography

Two families, two jobs, no exceptions:

| Role | Style | Reference faces (in order of preference) | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | High-contrast literary serif | Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, EB Garamond | Sentence case for headlines; all-caps + letterspacing reserved for the wordmark and section labels |
| Body / UI / prices | Quiet grotesque sans | Inter, Karla, Work Sans | Regular weight for body; medium for labels; never bold-shouting |

Hierarchy discipline: one display headline per surface; body text at 16–18 px equivalent with a line height of 1.6; prices always set in the sans, never the serif (prices are information, not poetry). Italics belong to the serif only, used for the world-words (*the hour, first light*) and titles.

## 6. Photography & Imagery

The single most important visual rule: **one fixed capture setup, one fixed grade, forever.** Consistency is what makes one artist look like a brand.

The grade is true golden-hour warmth: warm whites, soft side-light, gentle shadow. The subject canon, in priority order: close-crop eye work on clean skin; hands and tools mid-craft; window light in the studio; the reveal moment (client consent required, faces optional); the dog (in the lounge, never the lash room). Before/after images live in one fixed template — same angle, same distance, same light, umber frame on ivory ground, set names labelled in the sans.

Never: ring-light blue-white clinical shots, heavy filters, stock photography of models we didn't lash, collages, or more than one typeface on an image.

## 7. Materials & Applications

Phase 0 requires only four applications, produced without embellishment: the logotype lockup, the palette, one Instagram template set, and the aftercare card. Everything else waits for real content.

| Application | Standard |
|---|---|
| Aftercare card — "Keep the Light" | A6, Ivory Hour stock, Umber ink, gold rule; care instructions styled as a short morning-and-evening rite; The Return booking prompt on the reverse |
| Appointment card | Same stock; date/time in sans, one serif line: *your hour is held* |
| Labels (serum, cleanser retail) | Glyph + ORIANA + product plain-name; no ingredient poetry — clarity sells |
| Linen headrest cover | Sun-point glyph embroidered in gold thread, 20 mm |
| WhatsApp display | Glyph avatar on ivory; display name "Oriana · Private Lash Studio" |

## 8. Do and Don't — the Guardrails

**Do:** keep at least 40% empty space on every layout; let the photography carry warmth and the type carry authority; name real numbers (retention weeks, set counts) — proof is a brand asset; write prices plainly; keep the Korean-technique lineage in the craft story (About page, education posts) where it earns trust.

**Don't:** put "Korean" in the logo, name, or descriptor; use more than one accent-gold element per surface; discount publicly (the brand offers *The Return* pricing structure, not promotions); use urgency mechanics ("3 slots left!!"); use emoji in editorial surfaces (WhatsApp conversation is exempt — warmth beats purity there); ever show another studio's work.
