# ORIANA — Voice & Messaging Guidelines

**Document 02 of the Oriana Guideline Suite · Version 2.0 · July 2026**
**What changed in 2.0:** the voice is now built on Chesa's own words, taken from a founder interview. Version 1.0 sounded like a copywriter's idea of a lash studio; this version sounds like Chesa. When in doubt between an elegant sentence and a Chesa sentence, choose the Chesa sentence.

---

## 1. The Voice in One Line

**Small, soft, quietly confident — and lit up about it.** Not solemn luxury. Oriana's voice is a warm, cheery-quiet person who takes the craft seriously without taking herself too seriously. She is still learning her way, and says so; that honesty is the trust engine.

## 2. The Founder Voice Bank

These are Chesa's own words from the founder interview. They are the primary source for headlines, captions, and studio copy — quoted verbatim or lightly shaped, never "improved" into copywriter language.

| Her words | Where they now live |
|---|---|
| *"Even without makeup, what lashes do to me is light."* | The brand's central line. Hero copy, bio, About. |
| *"Not too much, not too little, just right."* | The styling philosophy. Menu page, consultation script, annotations. |
| *"It feels natural."* | The standard every set is judged against. |
| *"I love to control the mechanical pencil across the flow of paper."* | The artist origin. About page; the pencil-sketch motif in the visual identity. |
| Small, cuddly, soft, quietly confident, learning her way | The personality brief for every caption. |
| Quiet, calm, cheery, not too much — but always with warmth | The appointment promise, in her own adjectives. |

**The test for any new sentence:** read it aloud. If Chesa wouldn't say it across the lash bed to one woman, cut it.

## 3. The Three Registers

| Register | Where it lives | Sounds like | Sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Editorial** | Website, pinned posts, press | Chesa's philosophy, shaped for the page. Short sentences. Warm, not grand. | *"Lashes, to me, are light. Even without makeup. That's the whole idea."* |
| **Companion** | Instagram captions, Stories, Lemon8 | First person, honest about the craft and the learning; sparing emoji | *"Set forty-one. She asked for longer at the inner corner; I talked her into shorter. This is why. –C."* |
| **Care** | WhatsApp, aftercare, follow-ups | A friend who happens to be an expert; contractions welcome | *"Hi love — 48 hours in! How are they settling? Water's fine now, oil cleanser isn't. 🤍"* |

**The register rule:** never mix registers in one surface. Editorial never says "hi love"; Care never reaches for philosophy.

## 4. Vocabulary

**Her words** (use them; they are the brand): light · just right · natural · soft · quiet · warm · the hour · settle · keep the light · the Return · held · map/mapping · set.

**The craft-words** (use them; they build trust): natural lash health · retention · isolation · featherlight · lash-health-first · Korean-natural styling (education and About contexts only).

**The banned list** (never, in any register): falsies · spidery · mega volume · promo · cheap · "slay" · urgency mechanics ("last slot!!") · superlatives we can't prove ("Singapore's best") · any competitor's name · **and now also: over-polished copywriter abstractions** ("an arc of light," "the day's traffic," "a held hour" as gravitas). If it sounds like it was written at a desk instead of said across the lash bed, rewrite it.

## 5. Wubbles

The resident Cavapoo has a name, and the name is used. **Wubbles** appears in the Companion and Care registers freely (Stories, captions, WhatsApp arrival notes: *"Wubbles may say hello in the lounge — tell me if you'd rather he didn't"*), and on the Studio page of the website with his own portrait and caption. He is written as a character — small, curly, professionally cheerful — never as a policy line. The operational rules stay unchanged: greeting on request only, kept away entirely on request, never in the lash room.

## 6. Message Architecture

1. **The promise:** Lashes people assume are yours. (*Noticed, not announced.*)
2. **The philosophy, in her words:** Not too much, not too little, just right — because lashes are light, not makeup.
3. **The craft:** Every set mapped to the individual eye; featherlight, lash-health-first work in the tradition Chesa trained in.
4. **The person:** An artist since childhood — pencil and paper before tweezers and silk — quietly confident, still learning, honest about both.

## 7. Taglines and Sign-offs

Primary: **Noticed, not announced.** Rotational: *Lashes people assume are yours.* · *Not too much. Not too little. Just right.* · *Lashes are light.* Companion signs off **–C.** Care signs off with warmth, no formal signature. Editorial never signs.

## 8. Copy Patterns (steal these)

**Service description pattern:** world-name, plain descriptor, one Chesa-voiced sentence, price stated plainly. *"First Light — the signature set. Mapped to the lash line you already have, so it reads as yours. S$128."*

**Honest-refusal pattern (WhatsApp):** the no, the reason, the alternative. *"I won't put extensions on lashes this stressed — you'd lose them and blame the set. Let's do a lift and a serum plan first, and revisit in six weeks."*

**Rebooking ask pattern (in-studio, spoken):** *"Your Return window is the week of the 14th — Tuesday evening or Saturday morning?"* Always a choice of two, never an open question.

**The learning-out-loud pattern (Companion):** name the thing that went imperfectly and what it taught. *"Set fifty-three. I re-did the outer corner twice before it sat right. Worth it."* This pattern is unique to Oriana; polished studios can't copy it without breaking character.

## 9. Words About the Technique

The Korean lineage is evidence, not identity. It appears in exactly three places: the About page ("trained in the Korean-technique tradition of ultra-fine, lash-health-first work"), education posts, and the SEO layer (Google Business Profile, hashtags, alt text). It never appears in: the name, the logo, the descriptor lockup, headlines, or bio first lines.

## 10. Microcopy Standards

| Surface | Standard |
|---|---|
| Instagram bio | Line 1: the promise. Line 2: private one-client studio + area. Line 3: WhatsApp CTA. |
| Link-in-bio button | "Hold your hour" (primary) · "See the work" (secondary) |
| WhatsApp auto-greeting | *"Hi — you've reached Oriana. Tell me what your lashes do now and what you wish they did, and I'll take it from there. –C."* |
| Booking confirmation | Date, time, address with arrival notes, prep instructions, the Wubbles line, and one warm close: *"Your hour is held."* |
| 48-hour check-in | One question, one care reminder, no upsell. |
| Review ask (day 10–14, only after a compliment) | *"That means a lot — would you put that in a Google review? It's how a one-chair studio gets found."* |

---

*Write less than feels natural, state prices plainly, prove more than you claim — and say everything the way Chesa would say it to one woman in the chair, not an audience.*
