# ORIANA — Social Media Playbook

**Document 04 of the Oriana Guideline Suite · Version 1.0 · July 2026**

The operating manual for Oriana's social presence. Instagram is the primary stage; Lemon8 repurposes; TikTok waits. Everything here is sized to fit inside a 60–90 minute weekly batching session plus 90 seconds of capture per client set.

## 2. The Four Pillars

All feed content belongs to exactly one pillar. The mix is a monthly budget, not a daily straitjacket.

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Work** | 40% | Before/afters in the fixed template; week-three retention posts (*"three weeks in, still golden"*); mapping close-ups | Carousel (before → after → detail), single-image proof, 15–30s reels of the reveal |
| **The Craft Journey** | 25% | The 100-set counter; certification milestones; honest lessons (*"what set 60 taught me that set 30 couldn't"*) | Counter graphic + caption essay; timelapse reels; Stories polls on styling choices |
| **Lash Intelligence** | 20% | Education that saves lashes: aftercare myths, why isolation matters, styles explained (Korean-natural as a style among styles), who *shouldn't* get extensions | Carousel explainers (5–7 slides, one idea per slide); talking-head-free voiceover reels |
| **The World** | 15% | Window light, the scent, the ritual details, the dog, Chesa, the name's story told in fragments | Stories-first; occasional feed post when the image is exceptional |

## 3. Cadence

Sustainable beats ambitious. The rhythm: **3 feed posts per week** (roughly 2 Work / 1 rotating), **Stories most days** (even one frame — the studio, the light, the dog), **1 reel per week minimum** once the model era begins. Posting windows: SG lunchtime (12–1pm) and evening (8–9:30pm). Batching: capture continuously, produce weekly, schedule everything, then let WhatsApp interrupt-drive the rest of the week.

## 4. Templates & Visual Rules

Every recurring format has one locked template (built once in the brand palette, reused forever): the before/after frame (umber border on ivory, set name + week label in the sans), the counter graphic (large serif numeral, gold rule, ivory ground), the education carousel master (serif headline, sans body, ≤20 words per slide), and the quote/testimonial card (client words in serif italic, no stars, no name unless she offers it). Watermark: the sun-point glyph, bottom corner, 40% opacity. One typeface pair, one grade, no exceptions — the grid should look like one continuous photograph of the same warm room.

## 5. Captions

Companion register (Doc 02): Chesa's first person, warm, specific, signed **–C.** Structure for Work posts: one craft observation → one plain detail (set name, wear week) → quiet CTA (*"If this is the kind of natural you're after — link in bio, or WhatsApp us directly."*). Structure for Journey posts: the number → what changed → what's next. Hashtag policy: 3–8, in the first comment, mixing neighbourhood (#[area]lash), category (#lashextensionssg, #koreanlashsg, #naturallashextensions), and brand (#orianastudio, #firstlightset). Never in the caption body.

## 6. Stories Playbook

Stories are the world's daily rent. The five repeatable frames: (1) today's light — the studio window, no caption needed; (2) on the lash bed — a set in progress, mapping visible; (3) the reveal reaction (with consent); (4) the counter tick; (5) the dog's cameo (lounge only — never near the lash bed; the caption occasionally reminds: *"greeter, not technician — the lash room stays fur-free"*, which turns the hygiene question into a charm point). Interactive stickers weekly: styling polls ("longer inner corner or cat-eye lift?"), question box ("ask me anything about retention"). Every Story arc ends with the booking sticker or "DM us on WhatsApp."

## 7. DM & Comment Conduct

Comments answered within a day, warmly, by name where visible. Price questions in comments get a plain public answer (prices are public) plus an invitation to WhatsApp for mapping advice. DMs asking to book are moved to WhatsApp within two messages (*"So I don't lose you in DMs — WhatsApp is where the calendar lives: [link]"*). Never argue publicly; unhappy comments get one gracious public reply and a private follow-up. Competitor comparisons are never engaged.

## 8. Model-Era Specifics

Model calls run as Stories + one feed post, framed as **the model programme**: expectations stated (time, photos, feedback, punctuality), reduced pricing stated plainly, consent form linked. Every model set produces: before/after in template, one Story arc, one caption essay if the set taught something. The 100-set counter finale is a planned content event: the last practice set, the certificate, the menu reveal, and real pricing — one week of coordinated posts ending in "the golden hour opens."

## 9. What Never Appears

Other studios' work (even as "inspo"); client faces without written consent; the full home address (area only — exact address travels privately at confirmation); lash-shaming ("look how bad her old set was" — reframe as rescue stories with kindness); discounts, flash sales, countdown urgency; unproven claims; more than one emoji per Companion caption; anything that would look out of place in a warm, quiet, golden room.
