# ORIANA — Client Experience Guidelines

**Document 05 of the Oriana Guideline Suite · Version 1.0 · July 2026**

The service is the brand experienced at zero distance. This document scripts the client journey from first WhatsApp message to the week-three Return — the seven-step ritual, the studio standards, the policies, and the words. It exists so the hundredth client gets the same golden hour the first one did.

---

## 1. The Journey Map

| Stage | Touchpoint | Owner standard |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry | WhatsApp | Reply < 2 hours (9am–9pm); every conversation ends with a held hour or a clear next step |
| Confirmation | WhatsApp message | Date, time, address + arrival notes, prep instructions, photo direction request ("send a bare-lash photo in daylight if you can — I'll start mapping before you arrive"), *"Your hour is held."* |
| Reminder | WhatsApp, T-24h | Short; restates prep (clean lashes, skip the pre-appointment coffee); dog note with the opt-out |
| Arrival | The door | Greeted by name; shoes, water, the signature scent; phone basket offered — *"leave the day at the door"* |
| Mapping | The consultation | 5–10 minutes seated, never rushed: what her lashes do now, what she wishes they did, lifestyle (swimmer? oil cleanser? side sleeper?), then the map explained in plain words |
| The set | The lash bed | Quiet by default; music low; talking follows her lead; timing logged to the tracker |
| The reveal | The mirror | The mirror moment is protected — no chatter over it. The engraved line under the mirror: *noticed, not announced* |
| Send-off | The door | Aftercare card walked through (30 seconds, not a lecture); the rebooking ask as a choice of two: *"Your Return window is the week of the 14th — Tuesday evening or Saturday morning?"* |
| Check-in | WhatsApp, +48h | One question, one care reminder, no upsell |
| The Return | Week 3 | Infill priced to reward the rhythm; retention photographed (with consent) for the proof engine |

## 2. Studio Standards

**The room:** lit warm and low-angled like late afternoon regardless of the clock; Ivory Hour linens; the single signature scent (one scent, chosen once, never changed — scent is the most durable memory anchor); visible hygiene station. **The lash room is dog-free, always.** The Cavapoo is the lounge greeter and an advertised feature of the studio — with a genuine dog-free appointment option stated at booking for allergies and preference, no questions asked.

**Hygiene, stated not implied:** tools disinfected between clients in view or with process visibly documented; single-use where single-use is right; hands washed at the start of the mapping conversation, in front of her. HDB home-business registration and insurance noted quietly on the website and in the booking flow — trust infrastructure, stated once, never defensive.

## 3. Policies (published, warm, firm)

| Policy | Standard | The words |
|---|---|---|
| Patch test | Offered to all; required for sensitive histories, 48h ahead | *"Two minutes now saves a bad week later."* |
| Honest refusal | Extensions declined on over-stressed natural lashes; alternative always offered | The no, the reason, the alternative (Doc 02 pattern) |
| Lateness | 15-minute grace; beyond it, the set is shortened, not rushed | *"I'd rather give you a smaller perfect set than a full hurried one."* |
| Rescheduling | Free ≥24h notice; one same-day grace per client, then 50% fee | Framed around the empty chair, not punishment |
| Foreign fills | Assessed first; honest about what's salvageable | Never disparage the previous artist |
| Children / plus-ones | Not in the lash room (safety, insurance); lounge waiting acceptable | Stated at confirmation |
| Refunds | Retention issue within 72h: free fix, no interrogation. After: The Return pricing | The fix-first instinct is a marketing asset |

## 4. The Client Record

Every client has a card (digital) updated at every visit: the map used, curl/length/diameter, adhesive and humidity notes, retention observed at Return, lifestyle flags, preferences (music/quiet, scent sensitivity, dog opt-out), and the compliment log — what she said at the mirror, in her words. The record is what makes visit five feel remembered, and the compliment log feeds the review ask and testimonial engine.

## 5. Recovery Playbook

When something goes wrong — retention failure, a reaction, a client unhappy at the mirror — the sequence is fixed: listen fully without defending; fix first (free, fast, gracious); diagnose honestly (adhesive? aftercare? mapping?); log it; follow up at 48h. One genuine recovery creates more loyalty than ten flawless sets. What is never done: arguing, blaming her aftercare in the first conversation, or letting a fix wait more than 72 hours.

## 6. The Rebooking System

Rebooking is not a sales moment; it is the architecture. The rhythm is set at the send-off (choice of two windows), reinforced by the aftercare card's reverse ("your Return window: ___"), and rescued by a single WhatsApp nudge at day 17 if unbooked: *"Your set's about to hit its Return window — want Tuesday or Saturday?"* One nudge only. The target is 70% rebooking by end of Phase 2; the tracker logs every send-off ask and its outcome so the number is real, not felt.
