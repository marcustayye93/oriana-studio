# ORIANA — Photography Standards

**Document 06 of the Oriana Guideline Suite · Version 1.0 · July 2026**

Photography is Oriana's heaviest-lifting asset: it is the proof engine, the grid, the website, and the press kit. These standards exist so that every image — shot by one person on one phone in one corner of an HDB flat — looks like it came from the same warm, expensive campaign. The rule above all rules: **one fixed setup, one fixed grade, forever.**

---

## 1. The Fixed Photo Corner

One corner of the studio is permanently rigged for capture and never redecorated on a whim: positioned to catch real late-afternoon window light (the brand's actual production asset), with an Ivory Hour backdrop (linen or painted), the lash bed angle marked on the floor with tape, and the phone mount position fixed. A small warm LED panel (~3200–3500K) substitutes for the window on grey days, angled to mimic the same side-light. Nothing else enters the frame: no products-as-props clutter, no ring light ever (ring lights produce the blue-white clinical look the brand forbids, and telltale ring catchlights in the eye).

## 2. The Standard Shots

Every client set produces up to four assets in ~90 seconds:

| Shot | Spec | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Before** | Eyes closed, straight-down, 25–30cm, natural lashes clean and brushed, same tape-marked angle every time | The template's left panel |
| **After — closed** | Identical framing to the before (this identity is what makes the comparison credible) | The template's right panel |
| **After — open, three-quarter** | Eye open, slight three-quarter angle, focus on the lash line | The beauty shot; reels and covers |
| **Detail / macro** | As close as the phone resolves cleanly; the fan work and isolation visible | The craft-proof shot; education posts |

Retention shots at The Return reuse the exact before/after framing, labelled by week: *week 3 — still golden.*

## 3. Camera Settings & Craft

Phone camera rules: main lens only (never digital zoom past 2×); tap-to-focus on the lash line, exposure dragged slightly down to protect highlights; grid on, the eye line on the upper third; portrait mode **off** for lash work (fake bokeh eats lash tips); 4K/30 for reels footage. Hands and tools shots: mid-action, tweezers in frame, shallow natural depth. Consent governs every face: written consent (a line in the model/client form) before any identifiable image is captured, and the client sees the image before it posts.

## 4. The Grade

One preset, built once, applied to everything: warmth shifted golden (+8 to +12 on temperature), highlights softened, shadows lifted slightly, saturation restrained (skin must stay skin-coloured — never orange), no clarity/texture boosts on skin, no skin smoothing beyond what the lens does naturally. Faces are never retouched; lashes are never edited, extended, or filled digitally — **an edited lash photo is a lie about the product** and one discovered edit costs the entire proof engine. Save the preset in Lightroom Mobile (free tier suffices) as "Oriana Gold" and apply it to every image without exception.

## 5. Composition Language

At least 40% negative space in world/editorial shots; craft shots may fill the frame. Recurring motifs, in canon order: the eye and lash line; hands and tools; window light on linen; the studio threshold; the dog (lounge only). Horizontals stay horizontal; the camera is always deliberate, never candid-sloppy. Colour discipline in-frame: ivory, umber, skin, gold — a stray red towel ruins more than the towel.

## 6. Video

Reels use the same corner, same grade, same restraint: the reveal reaction (consented), 8–15 second mapping timelapses, slow pans across a finished set in window light. Captions burned in using the brand sans only. No trending-audio chasing that fights the world's tone — quiet or warm audio beds win; the occasional trend is allowed when it can be performed *inside* the brand's voice, not instead of it.

## 7. Library Discipline

Assets are filed weekly during the batching session: `/work/{set-number}_{style}_{week}` naming, best-of starred, consent status tagged. The before/after template, counter template, and carousel master live in one canonical folder and are never rebuilt ad hoc. Every quarter, the twelve best images are re-exported at full resolution into a running press kit folder — so when a listicle writer asks for images, the answer takes five minutes.

---

*The entire visual brand rests on repetition: same corner, same light, same grade, same frame. Resist every urge to vary it. The variety is the client's eyes; everything else is the constant that makes them the story.*
