# Award-site patterns applied to Oriana redesign (round 2)

## Patterns from Awwwards/D&AD winners relevant to a quiet luxury lash brand
1. **Type as image**: oversized display type (20-30vw scale moments), mixed italic/roman within one headline, words broken across lines deliberately. Typography IS the visual.
2. **Imperfections that add personality**: hand-drawn marks, misalignment, handwriting/annotation layers, human traces (pencil sketches — maps directly to Chesa's mechanical-pencil origin).
3. **Layering & floating**: images overlapping type, type running behind/in front of images, soft shadows, depth.
4. **Ultra-minimal navigation**, generous whitespace, fewer words — text as punctuation between images.
5. **Interactive storytelling**: scroll-driven reveals, marquees, hover states that feel alive. Keep under 300ms, subtle.
6. **Editorial art direction**: full-bleed imagery, asymmetric grids, big numerals, captions in tiny mono/caps.

## Redesign moves for Oriana
- Hero: massive display type with italic accent word + layered imagery; cut sub-copy to one line.
- Introduce a **handwriting/annotation layer** (Chesa's voice literally in the margins — "not too much, not too little, just right." as a script annotation). Use a script font (e.g., Nanum Pen / Caveat / Homemade Apple style — pick elegant, not childish).
- Pencil-sketch motif: generated pencil sketch of an eye/lash line — ties to her art childhood.
- Full-bleed image sections with 5-8 word statements instead of paragraph blocks.
- Wubbles gets a real portrait + personality caption.
- Marquee strip of her phrases ("light, not makeup · just right · one lash at a time").
- Word count target: cut each page's copy ~50-60%.
