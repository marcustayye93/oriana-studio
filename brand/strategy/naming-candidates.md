# Naming brainstorm — literary/mythological, placeless, Aesop-coded

Brief: reads as literary first (origin can exist but must not be the first read); mythological depth (option B); short-ish, wordmark-friendly; a little intrigue in pronunciation acceptable; category = lash/eye/beauty studio; must clear SG lash market, ACRA-plausible, IG handle plausible.

## Candidates (myth/literary figures connected to eyes, gaze, night, dawn, weaving, beauty)

1. **Nyx** — Greek primordial goddess of night. Short, striking. Risk: common brand name (Nyx Cosmetics = major conflict in beauty!). LIKELY BLOCKED.
2. **Selene** — moon goddess; soft, feminine. Risk: fairly common in beauty/aesthetic clinics.
3. **Aurelia** — Latin "golden"; literary. Longer; used by skincare brand Aurelia London. Conflict in beauty.
4. **Calliope** — muse of epic poetry. Literary, but reads whimsical/carousel.
5. **Iris** — goddess of the rainbow + the iris of the eye. PERFECT double meaning... but extremely common (florists, opticians). Weak ownability.
6. **Psyche** — mortal beloved of Eros; soul/butterfly. Beautiful literary weight; pronunciation intrigue ("sy-kee"). Risk: "psyche" mental connotation; check beauty conflicts.
7. **Ondine/Undine** — water nymph of European romances; literary (Giraudoux play, Fouqué novella). Soft, wave-like = lash "flutter/wave". Ondine = also a rare medical syndrome (Ondine's curse). Check.
8. **Vesper** — Latin "evening star"; literary (Bond's Vesper Lynd; vespers = evening prayer). Gender-neutral chic. Common-ish in hospitality/candles.
9. **Halcyon** — Greek myth kingfisher; calm seas; "halcyon days" = literary idiom. Evokes calm ritual. Longer word; check SG conflicts.
10. **Lumen/Lumine** — Latin light. A bit clinical/tech.
11. **Elowen** — Cornish "elm"; modern literary-feeling. Place-faint. Pretty but trendy-name vibe.
12. **Sirin** — mythological bird-woman (Slavic). Short, intriguing. Risk: reads like "siren" misspelled; Sirin = BlackBerry phone brand.
13. **Thisbe** — Ovid's lovers (Pyramus & Thisbe), the wall's crack — "seeing through". Deep-cut literary. Odd sound for beauty?
14. **Verity** — literary virtue word. Anglo, not mythic.
15. **Seren** — Welsh "star"; short, soft, chic. Faint place origin (Welsh) but reads abstract. Check SG usage (Seren = common SG spa/brand name? check).
16. **Noctua** — Latin "owl" (Athena's owl = wisdom, night sight, EYES). Intriguing, ownable. Slightly scientific (moth genus).
17. **Aletheia** — Greek "truth/unconcealment" — literally "revealing". Long.
18. **Oriel** — a window that projects light; also angelic name. "Window to the soul" adjacency. Short, rare, lovely wordmark. Check conflicts.
19. **Fable** — the most Aesop-adjacent English word. Literary category itself. Risk: Fable = game franchise, Fable London jewellery, many cafes. Crowded but category-distant?
20. **Solenne/Solene** — French given name, "solemn/ceremonial". Ritual connotation. Faint French read.
21. **Cassiopeia / Cassia** — queen of vanity placed among stars (myth of beauty!); Cassia = spice + botanical. Cassiopeia's myth is literally about boasting of beauty — interesting but negative arc.
22. **Lyra** — the lyre constellation; Orpheus's instrument; His Dark Materials heroine. Short, musical, literary. Common-ish baby name brandspace.
23. **Nereid** — sea nymphs. "NEREID" wordmark is handsome. Pronunciation intrigue.
24. **Eos** — dawn goddess (sister of Selene/Helios). 3 letters! Risk: EOS lip balm brand = beauty conflict. BLOCKED.
25. **Aria** — musical/literary. Overused.
26. **Sable** — soft dark fur; heraldic "black"; literary adjective. Lash-adjacent (dark, soft, fine hairs — sable brushes!). Strong candidate: sable brushes = fine-hair craft tool metaphor. Check conflicts.
27. **Aeon/Eon** — too tech.
28. **Mirabel/Mirari** — Latin "to wonder at/admire" root (miror = admire, mirror!). "Mirari" = rare, literary, the root of both "mirror" and "admire". Intriguing.
29. **Palimpsest** — too long/academic.
30. **Meridian** — literary-geographic. Used lots in wellness.

## Shortlist logic
Eye/gaze/night/fine-hair/beauty-myth relevance + rarity in SG beauty + wordmark quality + pronunciation intrigue:

- **Psyche** — myth of beauty & soul; Eros/Psyche is THE beauty myth; "the gaze that fell in love." Intrigue: sy-kee.
- **Vesper** — evening star; ritual hour; sultry-literary.
- **Halcyon** — calm-days idiom; the studio-as-sanctuary theme.
- **Oriel** — light through a window; eyes as windows.
- **Sable** — fine dark hairs, craft brushes; quietly perfect for lashes without saying lash.
- **Mirari** — Latin "to admire/wonder"; root of mirror; the reveal moment.
- **Fable** — max Aesop-coded; storytelling world.
- **Ondine** — water nymph, the wave/flutter.

Verify in SG: ACRA-style search proxies (Google "X singapore beauty/lash/studio"), IG handle plausibility, major beauty-brand conflicts.

### Round 3 outcome
Front-runner: **ORIANA** — oh-ree-AH-na. Soft (only liquids + open vowels), strong O lead, rolls off the tongue perfectly, literary-first: Oriana is the beloved of Amadís in the chivalric romance *Amadís de Gaula*, and the poetic name Elizabethan madrigalists gave their queen ("The Triumphs of Oriana", 1601 madrigal collection by Thomas Morley). Root: Latin *oriens* (rising, as in sunrise) / *aurum* (gold) — "the rising golden light." No SG beauty conflict found. Overseas conflicts are small local salons in India/Egypt — no SG registration, no digital collision at brand-build scale. Handle targets: @oriana.studio.sg / @orianalash / @oriana.sg; domain oriana.sg.
Theme continuity: "rising light / golden hour" world adapts the v2 "Evening Hour" theme to "The Golden Hour" — dawn-gold instead of dusk — OR keep dusk world and use Oriana's madrigal/court-poetry lineage ("sung, not spoken" ~ noticed, not announced).
Backups in order: Leto (needs verification), Oona, Halcyon (from round 2), Psyche (from round 2).
