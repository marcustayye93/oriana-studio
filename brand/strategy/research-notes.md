# Research Notes — Singapore Lash Market & Branding (July 2026)

## Market landscape (validated)
- Home-based lash salons are a normal, accepted category in SG; priced ~S$25–88 for full sets; premium studios S$98–198+.
- Dreamlash = the Korean premium benchmark; "Korean Classic Eyelash" from S$98; runs The Lash Academy (Korea Eyelash Association certification). IG: @dreamlashkorea — "Singapore's Trusted Korean Lash Boutique Since 2013 / Safe, Natural & Customized."
- J.Lashes: started home-based → became one of SG's most recognised boutiques via signature technique branding ("Tutu lash" designed for Asian eyes, 6 lashes per natural lash), from S$108. Proof that home-based can graduate to premium via a NAMED signature technique.
- The Cozy Studio (formerly The Lash Boss): home-based, famous for "YY Lashes," artist "Jess" personally known — personal-artist trust + signature style, from S$70. Home-based studio featured in Her World alongside premium salons = editorial validation possible.
- Private Room: influencer-favoured (Christabel Chua), from S$108 — influencer seeding works in this market.
- 3Lash: pet-friendly home studio is an existing, working differentiator in SG.
- Lashed Inc: appointments until 2am — convenience/schedule flexibility as differentiator.
- Dear Lash SG: lash-health-first positioning (cleaning, primer, bonder) — health angle exists at budget tier but not owned premium-ly by home studios.
- Most home studios named: generic "Lash + word" patterns (Lashed by Van, Lashie SG, L.ASHED, Celash, Dear Lash). Category is saturated with lash-puns; a Korean-language name (Gyeol) would be highly distinctive.
- Discovery channels: Instagram dominant; TheSmartLocal-style listicles; Lemon8 growing for SG beauty; WhatsApp = booking channel norm; Facebook groups (expats seeking consistent results = underserved segment).
- Pain points seen in market: inconsistent results (expat FB group complaint), pushy sales tactics at retail salons, heavy/spidery sets, lash damage.

## Strategic implications
1. Naming: Korean-language name (Gyeol) is genuinely white space vs. lash-pun saturation. Needs a descriptor lockup e.g. "GYEOL | Korean Lash Studio" for instant category recognition.
2. Signature technique naming (like Tutu, YY) is a proven premiumisation lever — Chesa should name her signature set.
3. Personal artist trust matters for home studios — hybrid: studio brand (Gyeol) + visible artist (Chesa) as face.
4. Pet-friendly is proven viable (3Lash) if advertised deliberately.
5. Lash-health-first + "lashes people assume are yours" promise = aligns with Korean natural trend, opposite of Russian volume cliché.
6. Editorial/listicle inclusion (Her World, TheSmartLocal, Daily Vanity) is an attainable PR channel for home studios.
7. WhatsApp booking is the norm; low-friction booking flow expected.

## Branding & content best practices (industry sources)
- Branding = what makes clients stay; marketing = what attracts them. Consistency across every touchpoint (IG, WhatsApp tone, studio decor, aftercare cards) is the core discipline.
- Signature style development is repeatedly cited: the thing clients rave about must be named, refined, and owned (matches SG evidence: Tutu, YY lashes).
- Professional branding elements checklist: logo, cohesive palette, typography, aftercare cards, branded cleanser/aftercare kits (white-label = extra revenue + retention tool).
- Brand story/authenticity: artist journey + mission on website/IG builds emotional connection — key for home studios where clients enter your home.
- Instagram playbook: portfolio feed w/ consistent lighting & close-ups; before/afters as core credibility; educational content (retention tips, myths); Reels favored by algorithm; Stories for last-minute slots; testimonials/reposts; local influencer collabs (free set for post); optimized bio with location + booking link; 3–5 posts/week + daily stories; 3–5 content pillars.
- Aftercare cards double as retention + brand recognition tools.
- Brand experience details: scent, warm blanket, calming ambiance — clients remember how they felt.

## Naming check — "Gyeol"
- No Singapore lash business found under "Gyeol" — the lane appears clear in SG lashes. Confirms the plan's lean.
- Adjacent uses exist elsewhere: "Brow Gyeol" (brow studio, Seoul), GYEOL STUDIO (hair, Busan, Korea), Gyeol Skincare (online K-beauty brand), Gyeol Haus skincare, Beauty Biotics Gyeol collagen. None in SG beauty services; none in lashes. Risk = moderate homonym crowding in K-beauty broadly, but category+geography distinct.
- Medium essay confirms the word's meaning is rich and translatable into brand story: "texture, grain, the natural direction of a strand" — perfect lash metaphor.
- Practical implication: secure handle variants like @gyeol.lash / @gyeolstudio.sg / @gyeol.sg; use descriptor lockup "GYEOL Korean Lash Studio" for clarity + SEO.
- Competitors using Korean angle in SG already: Serenity Beaute (Korean technique blog SEO), Bling Blink (K-DOLL lash lift), beautiful_soul.sg (home-based Korean lash). Korean angle is validated as growing but not yet owned by a single home studio brand.
